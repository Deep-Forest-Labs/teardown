SLUMBER Bestiary
PrototypesStudies
Mechanism studies: films, not playable. A source for geometry and stage concepts.

Each study is one real machine, its numbers checked in code, mapped onto what a stage or an enemy has to do. They are for picking elements to carry into the prototypes, not designs to build as they are.

Layer 1 · Sacrifice · after the National Theatre’s Olivier drum revolve

The Altar · Olivier drum revolve

The Olivier drum revolve (National Theatre): a drum turning, two elevator decks, a top half disc.

Worth lifting
  • Two decks that sink and rise inside a turning drum: a floor that reconfigures under the player
  • Counterweights you can see riding in the wall
  • One turning ring carrying everything else

Layer 2 · Sloth · after the Arecibo telescope

The Void · Arecibo

The Arecibo telescope: a 305 m spherical dish, a platform hung by cables from three towers, a turning arm and dome, tie-downs.

Worth lifting
  • Tiers taken from the dish’s own curve: the steps come out at 4 m and 8 m on their own
  • Belphegor as a platform hanging on cables from three towers
  • The feed beam through the sphere centre as the sleep beam: its reach is fixed by the 20° arm
  • Tie-down cables that reel outer platforms up into ramps
  • The 2020 collapse: the platform falling through the dish as phase 2
Its card in Prototypes
Layer 3 · Pride · after Fontana’s obelisk move

The Ruins · Fontana’s obelisk

Fontana’s move of the Vatican obelisk (1586): a timber castello, capstans, ropes, a sledge on rollers, an earth causeway.

Worth lifting
  • Pillars lowered on ropes onto the gallery edge as 34° ramps
  • The timber tower round Belial: a cage he stands in
  • Rollers running at half the load’s speed, carried round from back to front
  • Slabs dropping behind the rig, never under it
Its card in Prototypes
Layer 4 · Gluttony · after the grain elevator

Gluttony · grain elevator

A country grain elevator: piles at the angle of repose, a bucket elevator leg, a turning distributor, bins.

Worth lifting
  • Fly mounds as true repose cones (27°): they shrink exactly by what leaves them
  • A cyclone swarm round a head house on a tall leg
  • The distributor spout swinging to each bin in turn: the tell for which tower grows next
  • Towers that fill to double-jump heights (h = V/πr²), with the total volume conserved
Its card in Prototypes
Layer 5 · Greed · after the Lynton & Lynmouth cliff railway

Greed · water-balance lift

The Lynton & Lynmouth water-balance cliff railway: two cars on one cable, the top car takes on water and runs down.

Worth lifting
  • Lift pairs whose heights always sum to the same total: readable, predictable timing
  • Water ballast filling as the tell before a pair moves
  • One central headframe carrying every cable
  • Lock heights that climb both ways round the ring to the top lift
  • Mammon as the water tower feeding the cars, his tank the heart
Its card in Prototypes
Layer 6 · Lust · after Calatrava’s Brise Soleil

Lust · hydraulic flower

Calatrava’s Burke Brise Soleil (Milwaukee Art Museum): steel blades on hinges, each swung by a hydraulic cylinder.

Worth lifting
  • A bud that opens into six high platforms
  • Each petal on its own cylinder, so it can stop at any angle
  • Petal tips set at staggered angles as a spiral staircase to the bloom
  • Lilith rising on the central ram
  • Fans at the foot of the updraft columns

Layer 7 · Wrath · after the Panama Canal locks

Wrath · canal lock

The Panama Canal locks: a chamber filled and drained through culverts, mitre gates, locomotives on rack inclines.

Worth lifting
  • The fire as a lock’s fill: fast at first, then slowing (Torricelli), so the waves have a natural rhythm
  • Mitre gates meeting mid-yard with walkways on top
  • Amon as the towing locomotive climbing the rack incline
  • Galleries cantilevered off the cell-block walls

Layer 8 · Fraud · after Tower Bridge

Fraud · bascule bridge

Tower Bridge: two counterweighted bascule leaves on trunnions, turned by pinions on toothed quadrants.

Worth lifting
  • The mirror walls as raised bascule leaves
  • Leaves lowered past level onto the floor as ramps, then raised to meet mid-room as the new floor
  • Trapdoor tiles dropped in an order that always leaves one connected floor
  • Towers and high walkways framing the room
Its card in Prototypes
Layer 9 · Treachery · after the Finnish ice carousel

Treachery · ice carousel

The Finnish ice carousel: a disc cut out of a frozen lake and turned on the water by outboard motors.

Worth lifting
  • A lake already cut into rings, with the seams shut until phase 2
  • Floes at true freeboard: 8.3 cm of a 1 m slab shows
  • Rings drifting out by their own number of gaps and counter-turning
  • Motors set in the cuts
  • Dives that hold a floe under against its buoyancy
